What is BallotChain?

BallotChain is a general-purpose election and governance infrastructure engineered for institutional trust. From university student councils and faculty senates to corporate boards and labor unions, BallotChain replaces legacy, unverifiable paper and database ballots with mathematically verifiable blockchain records.

End-to-End Cryptographic Security

Every cast ballot is signed with asymmetric cryptographic keypairs and committed with individual cryptographic salts, protecting ballot integrity from casting to block inclusion.

Immutable Distributed Ledger

Accepted ballot transactions are grouped into batches and permanently sealed into SHA-256 blocks with proof-of-work. Historical blocks cannot be modified or reordered without detection.

Absolute Ballot Secrecy

Voter eligibility is verified securely during step-up OTP authentication, but individual voter identities are strictly unlinked from cast candidate selections, guaranteeing privacy.

Complete Audit Trail

Every administrative action, batch seal event, and result publication receipt is timestamped, attributable, and verifiable across the entire organizational lifecycle.

Strict Role-Based Isolation

Granular multi-tenant RBAC strictly isolates Platform Admins, Organization Admins, Election Officials, independent Auditors, Candidates, and eligible Voters.

Real-Time Operational Telemetry

Authorized officials monitor live consensus, batch occupancy, and voter participation rates in real time without compromising ballot confidentiality.

Controlled Governance

Governed Election Lifecycle

Elections transition through authoritative, state-gated lifecycle stages ensuring mathematical finality.

1
Draft Stage

Organization admins configure positions, register verified candidates, and establish the eligible voter roll. Voting is strictly blocked.

2
Open Stage

Voting opens. Eligible voters authenticate via step-up OTP, submit committed ballots, and receive immutable cryptographic transaction receipts.

3
Closed Stage

Voting is officially sealed. Remaining ballot batches are mined into final blockchain blocks with consensus-verified Merkle root hashes.

4
Published Stage

Election officials issue an immutable SHA3-256 publication receipt. Final tallies become publicly verifiable by voters and external auditors.
